If this is you, you are in good company

You might be 25 or 55. You might enjoy closeness and still feel like everyone else got a manual you didn't. Maybe you get close and then it just fades. Maybe you've started wondering whether you'd even recognise it.

Never having had an orgasm is common, and in most cases it is something you learn rather than something you lack. Sex educators and therapists have taught this for decades using a structured self-exploration programme. This workbook puts that programme into a short, private format you can start tonight.

The part most guides skip

A lot of women describe the same pattern: arousal builds, gets intense, and then they ease off or it slips away. Week 3 is built entirely around that moment.

What's inside

  • Week 1

    Ground

    What's really going on, the myths that keep women stuck, a short history page, and a no-pressure body map.

  • Week 2

    Map

    Guided solo sessions and a sensation log. What helps arousal build for you: time, breath, fantasy, lubricant, and an optional simple toy.

  • Week 3

    The edge

    When it builds and slips away. The "too much" feeling, the urge-to-pee feeling, letting go of control, and what to change (and not change) in that moment.

  • Week 4

    Take it with you

    A one-page "how I work" sheet, plus optional scripts for telling a partner what helps, without it becoming a performance review.

  • About 40 printable pages, written in plain British English
  • Sessions of around 20 minutes, three or four times a week

What this is not

An education and self-help workbook, not medical treatment. If you have pain, a change that started after a new medication or health condition, or difficult past experiences that come up during the exercises, please speak to your GP or a qualified psychosexual therapist. In the UK, the College of Sexual and Relationship Therapists (COSRT) keeps a directory of accredited practitioners.

There are no explicit images. Nothing in this workbook promises a result by a certain date, because bodies don't work to deadlines.
